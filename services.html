Our Services

We focus on surface finishes that hold up to real use. Every job starts with proper prep and ends with a finish that's built to last, no shortcuts, no upsell games.

Exterior Painting

Weather-ready coatings that protect siding, trim, and doors from Michigan's seasons while boosting your home's curb appeal.

Interior Painting

Clean lines, smooth coverage, and lasting color for every room in your home or business, with careful prep and protection for your floors and furniture.

Cabinet Painting

Full kitchen and bath cabinet refinishing, with proper degreasing, sanding, and a sprayed or brushed finish that looks factory-smooth and holds up to daily use.

Staining

Stain and seal decks, fences, and trim to bring out the natural grain and protect the wood against sun, moisture, and Michigan winters.

Caulking

Fresh, clean caulk lines around trim, windows, and countertops that seal out moisture and finish a paint job the right way.

Power Washing

Soft and pressure washing to strip grime, mildew, and old coatings before every paint or epoxy job, so the new finish actually bonds and lasts.

Drywall Repair

Drywall Repair

Patch, sand, and prep work that leaves walls and ceilings smooth and ready for a flawless paint finish, no visible seams.

Epoxy Floors

Seamless, durable epoxy systems for garages, basements, and commercial floors. Built to resist stains, hot tires, and heavy foot traffic without cracking or peeling.

Concrete Grinding

Mechanical surface prep that opens the pores of the concrete so epoxy and coatings bond properly and last.
