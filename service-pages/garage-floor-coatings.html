Garage Floor Coatings in Rochester Hills, MI

A garage floor takes more abuse than almost any other surface in the house: hot tires, dropped tools, oil and road salt, years of foot traffic. Epoxy seals bare or stained concrete into one durable surface that's far easier to keep clean than raw concrete, with a decorative finish that makes the space look finished instead of unfinished.

What's Included

  • Concrete grinding to remove old sealers, stains, and open the surface for adhesion
  • Oil stain and chemical spot treatment before coating
  • Repair of cracks and pitting in the slab
  • A base epoxy coat plus flake broadcast in your chosen color blend
  • A clear topcoat built to resist hot tire pickup and chemical spills

How We Do It

1

Inspect the Slab

We check for oil staining, cracks, and whether the floor has an existing sealer or coating that needs to come off first.

2

Grind & Prep

The concrete is mechanically ground to remove contamination and open the surface so the epoxy actually bonds, not just sits on top.

3

Repair

Cracks and pitted spots are patched so the finished floor is smooth and even.

4

Coat & Broadcast Flake

The base coat goes down and color flake is broadcast in for texture and a decorative finish.

5

Topcoat

A clear topcoat seals the system and adds resistance to hot tires, oil, and chemicals.

When You Need This

Oil and road-salt stains that won't scrub out, a garage floor that's never been finished, concrete that's started pitting or dusting, or an existing coating that's starting to peel are all reasons to consider a new epoxy floor. It's also a common upgrade when a garage doubles as a workshop or storage space and needs a floor that can actually take the wear.

Common Questions

Why do DIY epoxy kits peel so fast?

Most DIY failures come down to surface prep. Box-store kits are usually applied over concrete that hasn't been properly ground, so the coating sits on the surface instead of bonding to it and lifts under tire traffic.

How long until I can park in the garage again?

It depends on the coating system and conditions. We'll give you a clear timeline before the job starts so you can plan around it.

Can the color match my house or trim?

Flake blends come in a range of colors, so we can usually get close to a look that complements your house. Bring any color preferences to your estimate.

Will epoxy cover oil stains already in the concrete?

Surface staining is addressed during prep, and grinding removes the top layer of the slab where most staining sits. Deep, long-set stains may still show faint shadowing, but the new coating goes over a cleaned, ground surface either way.
