What's Included

  • Pressure or soft washing to remove dirt, mildew, and loose material before any paint goes on
  • Scraping and sanding of peeling or chalking paint
  • Caulking gaps at trim, siding seams, and joints to keep water out
  • Priming bare wood, patched spots, and problem areas
  • Two coats of exterior-grade paint on siding, trim, doors, and other painted surfaces

How We Do It

1

Wash & Inspect

We wash the exterior and walk the whole surface to find peeling paint, bare wood, and areas that need extra prep.

2

Prep the Surface

Scraping, sanding, and spot-priming bare or damaged areas so the new paint has something solid to bond to.

3

Caulk & Protect

We caulk gaps and cover or mask windows, fixtures, and landscaping before spraying or rolling begins.

4

Paint

We apply the coating in the number of coats needed for full, even coverage, working in sections so nothing gets rushed.

5

Final Walkthrough

We walk the exterior with you to check coverage and clean up the site before we call it done.

When You Need This

Fading color, chalky residue on your hand after touching the siding, peeling or cracking paint, bare wood showing at trim or corners, or a home that just hasn't been painted in a long time are all reasons to get it repainted. New siding and doors also need to be painted or sealed to protect the material from day one.

Common Questions

How many coats of paint do you use?

It depends on the surface and the color change, but most exterior jobs get two full coats over a primed surface for even coverage and long-term protection.

Do you paint in the winter?

Exterior paint needs the right temperature and dry conditions to cure properly, so we schedule exterior work around Michigan's weather rather than forcing it in poor conditions.

Will you paint over old, peeling paint?

No. Loose and peeling paint gets scraped and sanded first. Painting over it just means it fails again faster.

Do you paint brick and stucco too?

Yes, we paint most common exterior surfaces including siding, trim, brick, and stucco, with prep adjusted for the material.
