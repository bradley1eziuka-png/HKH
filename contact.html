Frequently Asked Questions

We're here to answer your most common questions about our coating and painting services.

Question 1: What types of coating and painting services do you offer?

We specialize in epoxy floor coatings, interior and exterior painting, power washing, drywall repair, and wood staining for homes and businesses across Oakland County.

Question 2: How long does it take to complete a project?

Most garage and basement epoxy floors are completed in 1 to 3 days. Painting projects vary by size, typically 1 to 5 days. We'll give you a clear timeline at your on-site estimate.

Question 3: Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. Every project starts with a free, no-pressure on-site estimate, so you know the price before you commit to anything.

Question 4: Do you offer references from past clients?

Absolutely. We're happy to share references and past project photos so you can see the quality of our work before you book.

We turn worn, damaged floors into durable, seamless finishes built to last, with honest pricing and clear communication from quote to completion.
