Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026

HKH Custom Coatings LLC ("HKH," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and our contact form, how we use it, and the choices you have, including around SMS text messaging.

Information We Collect

When you submit our contact form or request an estimate, we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, phone number, the service you're interested in, and any project details you share. We do not collect payment information through this site.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, schedule an on-site estimate, and follow up about your specific project. If you've provided a phone number, we may send transactional text messages related to your own request or appointment, such as confirming a scheduled estimate or following up on a quote. We may also email you about your request. We do not use your information to send unrelated marketing campaigns unless you separately opt in.

SMS Communications

By checking the SMS consent box on our contact form, you agree to receive transactional text messages from HKH Custom Coatings LLC at the phone number provided. Message frequency may vary based on your project. Message and data rates may apply from your carrier. You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message we send, or get help by replying HELP. Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing any service from us.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or share your phone number or personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Your information may be shared with our SMS/communications service providers solely to deliver messages to you, and with other trusted service providers who help us operate our business (such as scheduling tools), only to the extent necessary to serve you.

Your Choices

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any text we send you. You can also contact us directly at hkhcoatings@gmail.com to ask what information we have on file for you, update it, or request that it be removed, subject to any recordkeeping we're required to keep.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our business or communications practices change. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.
