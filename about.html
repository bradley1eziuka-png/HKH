Our Story

HKH Custom Coatings was built on years of hands-on experience. Hunter got his start working alongside his father's painting company, learning the value of proper prep, quality work, and taking pride in every job. That foundation became the standard for his own business.

Today, HKH is a small, owner-operated company specializing in residential and commercial painting, coatings, and epoxy flooring. Hunter is personally involved in nearly every project from start to finish, no handoffs, no corners cut, work he's proud to put his name on.
